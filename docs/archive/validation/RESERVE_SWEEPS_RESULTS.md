## HOW THE SWEEPS WORKED

### Capex Ceiling Mechanism

**Configuration:**
- Ceiling: 6 months of fixed costs (~$480K Year 30)
- Trigger: Monthly check if capex reserve > ceiling
- Action: Sweep excess to feeder prepayments

**Pattern:**
- Years 1-10: Capex reserve building (no ceiling hit)
- Years 11-30: Monthly sweeps as capex accumulates
- Peak sweep: $50K+ per month in later years
- Total swept: $2.82M over 30 years

**Impact:**
- Prevents runaway accumulation (was $3M+ without sweep)
- Maintains adequate capex buffer ($480K = 21 months coverage)
- Deploys excess to highest-yield use (debt reduction at 6.85%)

### Rainy Reserve Sweep Mechanism

**Configuration:**
- Target: 6 months fixed costs (rainy_months × fixed_monthly)
- Buffer: 120% of target (20% safety margin)
- Trigger: Reserve exceeds 120% of target
- Action: Sweep excess down to target (keep 6 months, redeploy buffer)

**Pattern:**
- Years 1-13: Reserve building, occasional dips below target during purchases
- Years 14-23: Reserve at target (no excess)
- Years 24-30: Compounding creates excess, monthly sweeps activated
- Total swept: $289K over Years 24-30

**Impact:**
- Maintains full 6-month emergency coverage
- Adds 20% buffer (7.2 months total) before sweeping
- Prevents interest compounding from creating idle excess
